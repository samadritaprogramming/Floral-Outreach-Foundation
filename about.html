Meet The founder

Angeline Nguyen is the founder of Floral Outreach Foundation, a purpose-driven floral business built on the belief that meaningful impact can begin with the simplest acts of kindness. Her journey into floristry began when she wanted to give a friend flowers but couldn’t afford to purchase a bouquet, inspiring her to create one using materials she found at home. What started as a creative solution soon developed into a passion for floral design and entrepreneurship. By combining artistic craftsmanship with a commitment to service, Angeline transformed her hobby into a mission-focused venture where every arrangement is designed with intention and every purchase helps support charitable initiatives. Through Floral Outreach Foundation, she hopes to demonstrate that beauty extends beyond flowers and aims to spread creativity, compassion, and generosity.
